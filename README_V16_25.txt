Engineer Islam Fouda Professional Base V16.25
- Added a separate Department Structure area for every department card, driven from the same central organization data.
- Department structures automatically roll up into the general company structure (single source of truth).
- Added one central place for adding people; available choices are dynamically restricted by the actor's company, department, hierarchy, and role.
- Department managers can add only inside their own department and only roles below their level.
- Engineers/foremen can only request additions within their own work scope and below their level.
- New people start as Pending Approval and do not become active until approved by Project Manager level or above.
- When a Project Manager approves, the addition is activated and a notification record is created for higher management.
- Permission ceiling remains enforced: delegated/default module access cannot exceed the requester/approver effective access.
- Existing Permit, Execution, Quality and other workflows are preserved; this version adds organization/approval behavior on top.
